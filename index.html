HOW TO USE
  1. Select your weapons by clicking on the weapon icons under "Selector"
  2. Click "Calculate" when all 5 weapons are added under "Current Rotation"
  3. "Weapons To Drop" tells you which weapons to drop. Drop your weapons in that order (left-to-right)
  4. Click the individual weapons on "Current Rotation" to remove from queue

Currently, only red-white rotation is supported.

WHAT IS THIS

This is a tool targeted towards new Aphelios players that sometimes go off their optimal gun rotation and can't find a way to fix it.

This tool does not find the quickest way to fix the rotation. Instead, it attempts to find an optimal way to get back into it. This ensures that players don't have to hold one weapon and burn out the other weapon just to fix their rotation, as doing so can be difficult.
